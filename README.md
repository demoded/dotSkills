# dotSkills
random set of skills for your clankers

### 🤖 Last.fm Weekly Roast Review 🎸🥁

    > **Turn your weekly music listening data into comedy, cosmic wonder, and ruthless conservatory terror.**

    An **Antigravity agent skill** that queries the Last.fm API for a user's 10 most played albums over the last 7
  days (or custom timeframes) and runs them through three distinct AI music critics:

    1. **🤖 The Robot-Bro**: An AI vibe-coder who drops album-by-album pun-heavy roasts and chaotic playlist
  summaries (`System.out.println('bruh')`).
    2. **🔬✨ The Curiosity-Bot**: A wonder-filled research bot celebrating eclectic genre-hopping through signal-
  processing hypotheses and exploratory joy.
    3. **🥁💥 Terence Fletcher (*Whiplash*)**: A brutal, abusive, and perfectionist jazz conductor demanding to know
  if you were rushing or dragging (`"There are no two words in the English language more harmful than 'good job'."`).

    ---

    ### ✨ Features
    - 📊 **Automated Last.fm Sync**: Fetches top albums, play counts, and artists via Last.fm API with UTF-8/Cyrillic
  support.
    - ⚡ **Chaos Detection**: Automatically flags multi-genre whiplash (e.g. symphonic metal + smooth vocal jazz +
  metalcore) with targeted wordplay.
    - 🐦 **Twitter-Ready Output**: Generates 15 ready-to-post 280-character tweets (5 summary roasts, 5 curiosity
  logs, 5 Fletcher evaluations).
